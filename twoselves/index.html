Two Selves

Your wanting self picks what to do. Your experiencing self lives through it. They disagree more than you think.

Before something, drag one dot: how much you want it, and how much you expect to enjoy it. Afterward, Two Selves checks back once and you drag again. Over a few weeks it maps where your expectations are wrong, in your favor and against it.

Free · iOS 18 or later · no account · your data stays on your phone

Two seconds before, two seconds after

No typing and no journaling. The line between your two answers is the gap, and the gap is the whole point.

Patterns you can trust

Two Selves only says something once your own check-ins back it up. Things like "you dread time with friends and enjoy it almost every time."

Catch the scroll

A Shortcuts automation can ask how much you want to open a feed app, then check back twenty minutes later. Step-by-step setup is in the app's Settings.

No streaks, no judgment

No red and green, no scores. Just a curious, honest look at what you actually like.

Private by default

Everything stays on your phone. No account and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
