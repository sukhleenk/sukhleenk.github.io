The short version: Two Selves doesn't collect anything about you. There's no account, no server of mine, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you put in the app stays with you.

This policy covers Two Selves for iPhone ("Two Selves", "the app"), made by {{ site.name }} ("I", "me").

What the app stores

When you use Two Selves you create moments:

  • a Kind (like Food or Friends) and an optional label;
  • your forecast: how much you want something and expect to enjoy it;
  • later, your report: how much you enjoyed it and how you felt afterward;
  • any notes you add.

The app also keeps your settings.

Where it's stored

Everything is stored on your device. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or your computer, your Two Selves data is included in that backup, which Apple protects and I can't access.

What I collect

Nothing. Two Selves has no account system, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs. I never see your moments, notes or patterns.

Calendar

If you turn on calendar suggestions (Pro), Two Selves reads the titles and times of your upcoming events on your device to suggest a Kind and a label. It never writes to your calendar and never sends event details anywhere.

Apple Health

If you turn on Save check-ins to Apple Health (Pro), each check-in is written to Apple Health on your device as a State of Mind entry: how pleasant it was, how you felt afterward, and a general area such as Friends or Work. Labels and notes are never written. Two Selves never reads any data from Apple Health, and health data is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Notifications and Shortcuts

Check-in reminders are scheduled locally on your device. If you hide labels on the Lock Screen, notifications and Live Activities show only the Kind. Shortcuts automations you set up, such as Catch the scroll, run on your device and only pass Two Selves the app name you typed.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. I receive no payment details. Apple may share anonymous sales information with me, as it does for all developers.

Your control

  • Export everything as JSON or CSV from Settings.
  • Delete everything from Settings, Your data.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device.

Children

Two Selves isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
